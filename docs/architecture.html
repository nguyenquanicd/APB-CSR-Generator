How an APB write/read becomes a CSR bit

A single-cycle SETUP-phase pulse (i_apb_psel & ~i_apb_penable) captures the address/write-data and kicks off a two-way req/ack handshake across the i_clk_apb ↔ i_clk_reg boundary — the register bits themselves live entirely in the reg_clk domain, and o_apb_prdata is resynced back to bus_clk using the same handshake pulse (the CDC fix from Revision 1.1) rather than a dedicated bit synchronizer.
